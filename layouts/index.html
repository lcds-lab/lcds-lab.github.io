Led by Wei Li

Building rigorous evidence for education decisions

The Longitudinal Causal Data Science Lab is directed by Wei Li, Associate Professor in Research and Evaluation Methodology at the University of Florida and NSF CAREER Awardee. Our work connects statistical methodology, causal evaluation, education data science, AI, and applied partnerships.

We work across two connected tracks: methodological research that develops statistical tools for stronger education studies, and empirical/applied projects that use those tools to answer policy and practice questions.

At a Glance

Two tracks, one evidence-building agenda

Methodological Research

We develop power, sample-size, causal inference, cost-effectiveness, and software tools for stronger education studies.

Empirical and Applied Evaluation

We study digital learning systems, literacy interventions, AI-enabled simulations, implementation, cost, and scale.

Projects create tools, training resources, and opportunities for students, postdocs, collaborators, and education organizations.
