Led by Wei Li

Building rigorous evidence for education decisions

The Longitudinal Causal Data Science Lab is directed by Wei Li, Associate Professor in Research and Evaluation Methodology at the University of Florida and NSF CAREER Awardee. Our work connects statistical methodology, causal evaluation, and applied partnerships in education.

Research Focus

Methods for learning from complex education data

Longitudinal Study Design

We develop design and analysis strategies for repeated measures, growth models, and multilevel longitudinal studies.

Causal Inference

We study experimental and quasi-experimental approaches for estimating causal effects in real education systems.

Education Data Science

We use large-scale administrative, digital learning, and assessment data to answer policy-relevant questions.

AI-Enabled Evaluation

We explore how machine learning and AI can support interpretable, equitable, and rigorous program evaluation.
