This page highlights selected funded projects and research initiatives connected to the LCDS Lab's four research focuses: longitudinal design and power, causal evaluation and heterogeneous treatment effects, education data science and AI, and intervention evaluation with cost-effectiveness and implementation evidence. Each project page collects the project overview, funding information, related publications, presentations, software, and external links.

## Selected Funded Projects

<div class="cards four">
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Longitudinal Program Evaluation with Lastinger Data</h2>
    <p class="meta">UF Lastinger Center | 2025-2026 | Principal Investigator</p>
    <p><strong>Focus:</strong> Longitudinal design, power, and applied program evaluation.</p>
    <p><strong>Problem:</strong> Help plan stronger longitudinal evaluations using realistic design parameters and advanced quasi-experimental methods.</p>
    <p><strong>Outputs:</strong> Design parameter evidence, examples for applied evaluations, conference presentations, and methodological manuscripts.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Sample Size and Power for Propensity Score Designs</h2>
    <p class="meta">UF HDOSE Strategic Reinvestment | 2025-2026 | Principal Investigator</p>
    <p><strong>Focus:</strong> Longitudinal design, power, and quasi-experimental planning.</p>
    <p><strong>Problem:</strong> Support sample size planning for propensity score-based studies with multilevel and longitudinal data structures.</p>
    <p><strong>Outputs:</strong> Methods development, simulations, and planning guidance for education researchers.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Causal Discovery and High-Dimensional Treatment Methods</h2>
    <p class="meta">UF HDOSE Strategic Reinvestment | 2026-2027 | Principal Investigator</p>
    <p><strong>Focus:</strong> Causal evaluation, heterogeneous treatment effects, and education data science.</p>
    <p><strong>Problem:</strong> Evaluate online learning platforms using high-dimensional data and methods that remain interpretable for education decision-making.</p>
    <p><strong>Outputs:</strong> Applied methods, analytic examples, and collaborative work with Lastinger Center data.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Machine Learning for Causal Heterogeneous Effects</h2>
    <p class="meta">UF HDOSE Strategic Reinvestment | 2022-2023 | Principal Investigator</p>
    <p><strong>Focus:</strong> Causal evaluation and heterogeneous treatment effects.</p>
    <p><strong>Problem:</strong> Compare machine learning methods for detecting heterogeneous treatment effects in multilevel randomized trials.</p>
    <p><strong>Outputs:</strong> Review, empirical comparison, presentations, and manuscripts on interpretable HTE estimation.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Multilevel Randomized Cost-Effectiveness Trials</h2>
    <p class="meta">NSF | 2020-2026 | Co-PI and Campus PI</p>
    <p><strong>Focus:</strong> Intervention evaluation, cost-effectiveness, and implementation evidence.</p>
    <p><strong>Problem:</strong> Develop statistical frameworks and tools for planning multilevel randomized cost-effectiveness trials.</p>
    <p><strong>Outputs:</strong> Peer-reviewed papers, PowerUp!-CEA software, presentations, and training resources.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Mediation Effects in Experimental Studies</h2>
    <p class="meta">Spencer Foundation | 2022-2023 | Principal Investigator</p>
    <p><strong>Focus:</strong> Causal evaluation, mediation, and optimal design.</p>
    <p><strong>Problem:</strong> Improve the design of experimental studies investigating mediation effects with individual-level mediators.</p>
    <p><strong>Outputs:</strong> Methodological article, design guidance, and conference presentations.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Scaling Targeted Reading Instruction</h2>
    <p class="meta">IES | 2022-2025 | Co-Principal Investigator</p>
    <p><strong>Focus:</strong> Intervention evaluation, cost data, and implementation evidence.</p>
    <p><strong>Problem:</strong> Study the digital implementation and scaling of an effective reading intervention for students impacted by COVID-19.</p>
    <p><strong>Outputs:</strong> Cost data collection and analysis, evaluation evidence, and collaborative dissemination.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="card">
    <h2>CRISIS: Counselor Response and Intervention Simulation</h2>

    <p><strong>Focus:</strong> Education data science, AI, and simulation-based learning systems.</p>
    <p><strong>Problem:</strong> Develop and evaluate intelligent support for counselor response and intervention simulation.</p>
    <p><strong>Outputs:</strong> AI-enabled learning system evidence, collaborative research products, and future grant-development capacity.</p>
