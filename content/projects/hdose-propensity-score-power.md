This HDOSE Strategic Reinvestment project, **Sample Size and Power Analysis Methods for Propensity Score-Based Quasi-Experimental Studies with Multilevel and Longitudinal Designs**, develops planning methods for quasi-experimental education research.

The project is part of the lab's [Longitudinal Design, Power, and Optimal Study Planning](/research/#longitudinal-design-power-and-optimal-study-planning) focus area.

## Project Information

- **Funding source:** University of Florida HDOSE Strategic Reinvestment Fund
- **Role:** Principal Investigator
- **Co-PI:** Walter Leite
- **Project period:** July 1, 2025-June 30, 2026
- **Award amount:** $14,984

## Project Focus

- Develop power and sample-size methods for propensity score-based quasi-experimental studies.
- Address multilevel and longitudinal design structures.
- Support researchers planning observational impact studies in education.
