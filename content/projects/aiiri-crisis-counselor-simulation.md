**CRISIS: Counselor Response and Intervention Simulation with Intelligent Support** is a UF AIIRI SEED Fund project focused on intelligent support for counselor response and intervention simulation.

Within the lab, this project extends education data science and AI work into simulation-based counselor training and intervention support. It is an interdisciplinary project where the lab contributes quantitative, methods, and evaluation expertise.

## Project Information

- **Funding source:** University of Florida Artificial Intelligence & Informatics Research Institute SEED Fund
- **Role:** Project team
- **PI/Co-PIs:** Yusen Zhai, Benjamin Lok, Laura Shannonhouse, Yung-Wei Dennis Lin, Melissa Mariani, Wei Li
- **Project period:** May 1, 2026-April 30, 2027
- **Award amount:** $47,000

## Project Focus

- Develop and study intelligent support for counselor response and intervention simulation.
- Connect AI-enabled simulation with applied education and counseling contexts.
- Contribute quantitative, methods, and evaluation expertise to the interdisciplinary project team.
