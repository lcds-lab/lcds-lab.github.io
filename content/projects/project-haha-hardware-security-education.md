**Project HaHa: Low-Cost Hands-on Hardware Security Education** supported research and development around accessible hardware security learning experiences.

The project contributes to the lab's broader work on applied education evaluation, STEM learning, and technology-supported education.

## Project Information

- **Funding agency:** Florida Center for Cybersecurity
- **Role:** Co-Principal Investigator
- **PI/Co-PIs:** Wanli Xing, Swarup Bhunia, Sandip Ray, Wei Li
- **Project period:** June 1, 2022-August 31, 2023
- **Award amount:** $289,168

## Project Focus

- Support low-cost hands-on learning in hardware security education.
- Contribute quantitative and evaluation expertise to a STEM education project.
- Generate evidence to inform technology-rich instructional design.
