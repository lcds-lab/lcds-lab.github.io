This internal research-methods project, **Causal Discovery and High-Dimensional Treatment Methods for Evaluating Online Learning Platforms: With Application to Lastinger Center Literacy Matrix Data**, supports methodological work for evaluating digital learning environments.

Within the lab, this project connects causal methods with large-scale digital learning data. The applied setting is Lastinger Center Literacy Matrix data, with methodological emphasis on causal discovery, high-dimensional treatments, and interpretable evaluation of online learning platforms.

## Project Information

- **Funding source:** University of Florida HDOSE Strategic Reinvestment Fund
- **Role:** Principal Investigator
- **Co-PI:** Katherine J. Strickland
- **Project period:** July 1, 2026-June 30, 2027
- **Award amount:** $15,000

## Project Focus

- Develop and apply causal discovery methods for online learning platform evaluation.
- Study high-dimensional treatment methods in education data.
- Use Lastinger Center Literacy Matrix data as an applied setting for methods development.
