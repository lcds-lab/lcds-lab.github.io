This University of Alabama Research Grants Committee project, **Power Analysis for Multilevel Randomized Cost-Effectiveness Trials with Binary Effectiveness Measures**, supported early methodological work on cost-effectiveness trial planning.

The project is part of the lab's [Intervention Evaluation, Cost-Effectiveness, and Implementation Evidence](/research/#intervention-evaluation-cost-effectiveness-and-implementation-evidence) focus area.

## Project Information

- **Funding source:** University of Alabama Research Grants Committee
- **Role:** Principal Investigator
- **Project period:** May 15, 2018-May 14, 2020
- **Award amount:** $5,964

## Project Focus

- Study power analysis for multilevel randomized cost-effectiveness trials.
- Address binary effectiveness measures.
- Contribute to the foundation for later cost-effectiveness design tools.
