**Democratizing High-Quality Course Sequence Knowledge** used education data to support stronger understanding of course pathways and student progress.

The project is part of the lab's [Education Data Science, AI, and Digital Learning Systems](/research/#education-data-science-ai-and-digital-learning-systems) focus area.

## Project Information

- **Funding agency:** Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation
- **Role:** Co-Principal Investigator
- **PI/Co-PIs:** Justin Ortagus, Ben Skinner, Wei Li
- **Project period:** 2022-2024
- **Award amount:** $509,824

## Project Focus

- Study course-sequence knowledge using education data.
- Support evidence for student pathways and postsecondary decision-making.
- Contribute quantitative and education-data expertise to the project team.

## Note

Wei Li assumed the Co-PI role in spring 2024 after Dr. Ben Skinner transitioned from UF.
