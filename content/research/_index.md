The LCDS Lab develops and applies rigorous methods for studying education programs, policies, and learning systems over time.

<div class="cards four">
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Longitudinal Designs</h2>
    <p>We study repeated measures, growth trajectories, cluster randomized designs, multisite designs, and longitudinal power analysis.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Causal Inference</h2>
    <p>We develop and apply experimental, quasi-experimental, propensity score, moderation, mediation, and causal machine learning methods.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Education Data Science</h2>
    <p>We work with administrative, assessment, online learning, and intervention data to support interpretable and policy-relevant evidence.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="card">
    <h2>Evaluation and Cost-Effectiveness</h2>
    <p>We design studies that connect impact, implementation, cost, and scalability for education programs and interventions.</p>
  </article>
</div>

## Selected Funded Work

- NSF CAREER: Statistical Power Analysis and Optimal Sample Size Planning for Longitudinal Studies in STEM Education.
- Statistical framework and tools for planning multilevel randomized cost-effectiveness trials.
- Enhancing longitudinal program evaluations using Lastinger Center data.
- AI- and machine-learning-supported evaluation of educational programs and digital learning environments.
