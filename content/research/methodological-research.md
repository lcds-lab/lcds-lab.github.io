The LCDS Lab's methodological research portfolio develops statistical tools, study-design guidance, software, and training resources for education research. These projects focus on longitudinal design, power and sample-size planning, causal inference, heterogeneous treatment effects, mediation, cost-effectiveness, and related methods.
