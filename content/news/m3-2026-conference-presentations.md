Wei Li and Katherine J. Strickland presented collaborative work at the **2026 Modern Modeling Methods Conference**.

- On June 23, Katherine Strickland and Wei Li presented **"Power Analysis for Difference-in-Differences Studies with Staggered Treatment Adoption"** in the Modern Approaches to Power Analysis session.
- On June 24, Katherine Strickland, Jennifer Hill, Ying Lu, and Wei Li presented **"Estimating Heterogeneous Effects of the Gifted and Talented Program Using Bayesian Additive Regression Trees"** in the Educational Applications of Modern Modeling Methods session.

The full program is available from the [Modern Modeling Methods conference website](https://modeling.fordham.edu/mmm-2026-full-program/).
