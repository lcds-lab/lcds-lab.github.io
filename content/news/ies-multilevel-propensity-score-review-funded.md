---
title: "IES Funds Multilevel Propensity Score Systematic Review"
date: 2026-08-01
description: "An IES methodological innovation award will support a systematic review and practical guidance for propensity score analysis with multilevel educational data."
---

The Institute of Education Sciences has funded **Propensity Score Analysis of Multilevel Data: A Systematic Review** through its Statistical and Research Methodology in Education program. Walter Leite is Principal Investigator, with Huibin Zhang and Wei Li serving as Co-Principal Investigators. The two-year University of Florida project (R305D260024) received $348,917 to develop a systematic review, training courses, flowcharts, checklists, and video resources for education researchers.

[Read about the lab project](/projects/ies-multilevel-propensity-score-review/) or visit the [official IES award page](https://ies.ed.gov/use-work/awards/propensity-score-analysis-multilevel-data-systematic-review).
