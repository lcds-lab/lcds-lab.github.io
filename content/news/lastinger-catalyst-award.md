title: "Longitudinal Program Evaluation Project Receives Lastinger Catalyst Award"
date: 2025-04-24
description: "Wei Li's project on longitudinal program evaluation and design parameters received funding from the COE-Lastinger Research Catalyst Awards."

Wei Li's project, "Enhancing Longitudinal Program Evaluations: Estimating Design Parameters and Illustrating Advanced Quasi-Experimental Methods Using UF Lastinger Center Data," received funding from the [2025-26 COE-Lastinger Research Catalyst Awards](https://education.ufl.edu/news/2025/05/08/lastinger-center-for-learning-funds-four-college-of-education-faculty-projects/).
