LCDS Lab research was featured at the **2026 Society for Research on Educational Effectiveness (SREE) Conference**, held September 23-26 in Baltimore, Maryland. The program included three presentations with **Wei Li** or **Katherine J. Strickland (Kat)** as the designated presenter, alongside a collaborative paper presented by **Wendy Chan**.

## Difference-in-Differences and Study Planning

**Difference-in-Differences Under Conditional Parallel Trends With Time-Invariant Covariates: Methods, Implementation, and Power Analysis**

- **Authors:** Wei Li and Katherine J. Strickland.
- **Presenter:** Wei Li.
- **Session:** Quasi-Experimental and Non-Experimental Designs, September 25.

This methodological work connects difference-in-differences under conditional parallel trends to implementation and statistical power analysis for study planning.

## Neighborhoods in Multilevel Research

**Neighborhoods as a Level, Not Just a Covariate: A Framework for Urban School Research Using Philadelphia and New York City**

- **Authors:** Katherine J. Strickland and Wei Li.
- **Presenter:** Katherine J. Strickland.
- **Session:** Methods of Addressing Multi-Level Data, September 23.

This work considers neighborhoods as a distinct level in the design and analysis of urban school research, rather than treating neighborhood characteristics only as covariates.

## Access to Gifted Education

**The Geography of Gifted Education: School and Neighborhood Predictors of Access to Gifted and Talented Programs in New York City**

- **Authors:** Katherine J. Strickland, Wendy Chan, Michael Gottfried, and Sam K. Peters.
- **Presenter:** Katherine J. Strickland.
- **Session:** Advanced Learning Access, Equity, and Scaled Instructional Models, September 25.

This applied study examines school and neighborhood predictors of access to gifted and talented programs in New York City.

## Additional Collaborative Research

**The Transportability of Impacts in New York City's Gifted and Talented Program**

- **Authors:** Wendy Chan, Nowreen Yasmin, Michelle Zhou, and Katherine J. Strickland.
- **Presenter:** Wendy Chan.
- **Session:** Causal Inference in Practice, September 25.

This collaborative work examines the transportability of program impacts, connecting causal evaluation with questions about how findings extend to other settings or populations.
