Wei Li (**Principal Investigator**) and Katherine J. Strickland (**Co-PI**) received external applied-study funding through the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation AIMS EduData Initiative, University of Florida Lastinger Center track.

The project, **"Interpretable Teacher Engagement Profiles and Causal Effects in Math Matrix Micro-Credentials,"** runs from June 1, 2026 to August 31, 2026 and supports work on teacher engagement profiles and causal effects in Math Matrix micro-credentials.
