Wei Li's proposal, "Sample Size and Power Analysis Methods for Propensity Score-Based Quasi-Experimental Studies with Multilevel and Longitudinal Designs," was selected for funding by the HDOSE 2025 Strategic Reinvestment Program. The project supports new methods and tools for sample size planning in propensity score analyses involving nested data.
