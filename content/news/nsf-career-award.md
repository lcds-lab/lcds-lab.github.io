---
title: "NSF CAREER Award Funded"
date: 2024-08-01
description: "Wei Li received an NSF CAREER award to develop methods and tools for power and sample size planning in longitudinal studies."
---

Wei Li's NSF CAREER proposal was funded as a five-year project with a total budget of $1.24 million. The project develops new methods and tools for statistical power and sample size computations in the design of longitudinal experimental and quasi-experimental studies. The award abstract is available from [NSF](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2339353).
