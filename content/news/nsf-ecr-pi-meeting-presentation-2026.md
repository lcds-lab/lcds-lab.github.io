Wei Li will attend the **2026 ECR:Core and ECR:BCSER Awardee Meeting** and present **"Statistical Power and Design Planning for Longitudinal STEM Studies"** in the lightning roundtable session **Longitudinal Research on Early STEM Learning**.

The session is scheduled for June 30, 2026, from 10:45 AM to 12:00 PM ET. The presentation highlights the lab's NSF CAREER work on statistical power analysis, optimal design, and sample-size planning for longitudinal STEM education studies.
