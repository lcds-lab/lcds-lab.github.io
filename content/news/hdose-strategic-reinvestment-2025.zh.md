Wei Li 的提案 **Sample Size and Power Analysis Methods for Propensity Score-Based Quasi-Experimental Studies with Multilevel and Longitudinal Designs** 获得 HDOSE 2025 Strategic Reinvestment Program 资助。项目支持嵌套数据中倾向得分分析样本量规划方法和工具的发展。
