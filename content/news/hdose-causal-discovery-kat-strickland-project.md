Wei Li (**Principal Investigator**) and Katherine J. Strickland (**Co-PI**) received internal University of Florida HDOSE Strategic Reinvestment Fund support for a research-methods project.

The project, **"Causal Discovery and High-Dimensional Treatment Methods for Evaluating Online Learning Platforms: With Application to Lastinger Center Literacy Matrix Data,"** runs from July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027 and supports methodological work for evaluating online learning platforms using high-dimensional data.
