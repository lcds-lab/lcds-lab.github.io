Wei Li joined the project team for **CRISIS: Counselor Response & Intervention Simulation with Intelligent Support**, a University of Florida Artificial Intelligence & Informatics Research Institute (AIIRI) SEED Fund project. The project, with Yusen Zhai, Benjamin Lok, Laura Shannonhouse, Yung-Wei Dennis Lin, Melissa Mariani, and Wei Li, focuses on intelligent support for counselor response and intervention simulation.
